HoopTracker guide

Everything stays on your device. Use this page for quick command reminders, stat tips, and session setup before you hit the court.

Quick start
  1. Pick a shot type on the main screen.
  2. Tap Start Voice Tracking or record shots manually.
  3. Say hit, miss, or add a type like free throw hit.
  4. Open History or Settings anytime from the top bar.
The selected shot type now updates both tracking and the stats shown on the dashboard.

Voice commands

hit make made good miss brick undo

Add a shot type in the same phrase for more specific tracking:

  • three pointer hit
  • mid range miss
  • layup make
  • free throw miss

Stats and goals

The dashboard updates for the selected time range and shot type.

  • Today shows your current day totals.
  • Week and Month include trend charts.
  • Year gives a longer summary view.
Daily goals show progress for both shot volume and shooting percentage. Update them in Settings and watch the progress card move in real time.

History and undo

  • Every recorded shot appears in Shot history with its type and timestamp.
  • Swipe left or right to delete a shot.
  • Use the in-app or notification Undo action to restore the most recent shot if you recorded something by mistake.
Deleted history items can only be restored immediately from the undo action.

Helpful settings

Haptic feedback gives a quick vibration after each result.
Voice feedback speaks the last outcome aloud.
Auto-start session creates a practice session as soon as voice tracking starts.
Daily goals shape the dashboard progress targets.

Track honestly, practice consistently, and use the trends to spot what needs work next.
