Privacy policy

HoopTracker is designed so everything you do stays on your device. Nothing leaves.

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy explains how HoopTracker handles your information. Because we do not collect or share anything, the policy is intentionally short.

Data we do not collect

HoopTracker does not collect, store on external servers, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information.

On-device permissions

Microphone

HoopTracker uses your device microphone for on-device voice command recognition (detecting hit, miss, and shot-type keywords). All audio processing happens locally using Android speech recognition. No audio recordings are saved, stored, or transmitted off your device.

Notifications

HoopTracker shows a persistent notification while voice tracking is active so you can quickly stop tracking or undo shots. This notification never contains personal data and is managed entirely on-device.

Bluetooth

HoopTracker does not directly use Bluetooth. However, if you connect Bluetooth headphones, the voice feedback feature may route audio through them. No Bluetooth data is collected or transmitted by the app.

Local storage

All shot records, preferences, and session data are stored exclusively in the app's private local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use the Clear All Data option in Settings.

No internet, no analytics, no ads

HoopTracker has zero internet access. The app does not request the internet permission.
  • No analytics or crash reporting frameworks
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party services or SDKs that communicate externally
  • No user accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync

Open source

HoopTracker is open source. You can review the full source code to verify exactly how your data is handled.

Children's privacy

HoopTracker does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected at all, there is no risk of accidental collection from minors.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out through the project's open-source repository.

No data ever leaves your device. That is our commitment.
